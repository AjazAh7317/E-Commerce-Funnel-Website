01 / THE CONTEXT

Turning customer
activity into answers.

The business had a customer journey to understand: what attracts visitors, where they hesitate and which marketing channels translate attention into purchases.

Using more than 120,000 customer interaction records, I explored funnel behaviour, marketing performance, devices and regional patterns. The analysis combined data preparation, SQL, exploratory and statistical analysis, and a three-page Power BI dashboard.

03 / WHAT THE DATA SUGGESTS

Five observations.
Five opportunities.

01

Email marketing stood out.

Email delivered the highest reported ROI (32,000%) and the lowest customer acquisition cost among the analysed channels. This points to an opportunity to test scaling email while monitoring profitability and customer quality.

32,000%Reported email ROI
02

The cart-to-checkout journey needs attention.

The largest reported customer drop-off was between the cart and checkout stages. Investigate unexpected costs, checkout complexity, payment issues and usability friction.

Cart →Checkout focus
03

Mobile conversion was slightly lower.

Mobile users converted slightly less often than desktop users. Review mobile navigation, page performance, checkout and payment experience.

MobileExperience opportunity
04

Bangalore and Delhi performed comparatively well.

These locations showed stronger conversion performance in the analysis. Explore targeted campaigns and validate the pattern across time and customer segments.

2 regionsBangalore · Delhi
05

Instagram traffic alone isn't the outcome.

Instagram generated considerable traffic but lower ROI than email. Review targeting and campaign quality with conversion and profitability—not traffic alone—in mind.

ROI firstEvaluate campaign quality

Findings and figures reflect the analysis described in this project. Recommendations are opportunities to investigate—not verified outcomes after implementation.
